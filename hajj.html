Hajj services

Hajj is prepared early — and through the right channels.

Hajj registration, quotas and visas are managed by the Saudi authorities and each country’s national Hajj authority. Here is how the roles are divided.

01

Official registration

In Nigeria, Hajj is regulated by the National Hajj Commission of Nigeria (NAHCON), state pilgrims’ welfare boards and licensed private tour operators. Elsewhere, the national Hajj authority or the official Nusuk platform applies, depending on country of residence.

02

Authorised packages

Hajj packages can only be provided by operators authorised for the relevant country. We will tell you in writing which operator or official body is responsible for your registration and package.

03

Our assistance

Guidance on steps and deadlines, document preparation, health requirements, flight and logistics coordination, and regular communication with you and your family.

Important: an enquiry with AL MANARAH does not guarantee a Hajj place or a Hajj visa. We are not an official Hajj registration platform. Never pay Hajj fares to an unverified individual or agency — always verify with your country’s official authority.
