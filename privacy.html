Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how AL MANARAH GLOBAL SERVICES (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use this website, contact us or use our services.

1. Who we are

AL MANARAH GLOBAL SERVICES, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria (RC 9306173), business address: No. 13, Adjame Bromakote, à proximité de l’école Saint Bernard, Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire. Contact for privacy matters: almanarahglobalservices@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry information: name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, country of residence, the service you are interested in, travel dates, departure city, number of travellers, room and package preferences, and your message.
  • Partner information: organisation name, contact person, location, contact details and business description.
  • Booking and visa information (only once you proceed): passport details and copies, photographs, dates of birth, nationality, residence details and other documents required by airlines, hotels or authorities. These are requested only through a secure channel we confirm with you in writing — never through this website.
  • Payment records: amounts, dates, receipts and payment references. We do not collect card numbers through this website.
  • Health information: where a destination or Hajj authority requires it (for example vaccination certificates), only to the extent required.

When you submit an enquiry or partner form, it is stored in our enquiry-management system (a Google Sheets workspace accessible only to authorised AL MANARAH staff) and given a reference number. We use it to record your enquiry status, quotations, payments and booking progress. The “Track your enquiry” page shows only your status, booking progress, payment status (no amounts) and our latest note, after you enter both your reference number and phone number. The website does not accept file uploads. If you choose WhatsApp or email instead, your message is only sent when you confirm it in that application.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare quotations (steps taken at your request before a contract).
  • To arrange the services you book — flights, hotels, transport, visas and pilgrimage support (performance of a contract).
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations (legal obligation).
  • To manage partner relationships and prevent fraud (legitimate interests).
  • To send you updates or offers only if you ask for them (consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

4. Who may process your information

We share only what is needed to provide the service you requested, with:

  • Communication and storage providers: WhatsApp (Meta) and Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive for quotations and receipts), and Cloudflare (Turnstile anti-spam check), under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Service providers you book through us: airlines and ticketing providers, hotels, transport operators, the Haramain railway, authorised Umrah and Hajj service companies.
  • Official bodies: Saudi visa and Hajj platforms and authorities, and national Hajj authorities where applicable.
  • Our website hosting provider [name of hosting provider], and — if enabled — our form-processing provider [name].
  • Authorities where required by law.

Travel to Saudi Arabia necessarily involves transferring your information to Saudi Arabia and possibly other countries. We transfer only what the service requires.

5. How we store and protect it

We limit access to staff who need the information, use password-protected accounts with two-step verification, and delete documents that are no longer needed. Identity documents are received only through a secure channel confirmed in writing. 6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: up to [12 months].
  • Passport copies and visa documents: deleted within [90 days] after your trip ends, unless the law requires longer.
  • Booking, invoice and payment records: for the period required by accounting and tax law [confirm, e.g. 6 years].

7. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or receive a copy. Write to almanarahglobalservices@gmail.com; we will reply within the time required by applicable law. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

8. Applicable laws

We aim to comply with the data-protection laws that apply to our activities, which may include the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (Nigeria Data Protection Commission), Côte d’Ivoire Law No. 2013-450 on the protection of personal data (supervised by ARTCI), and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law where it applies to processing in Saudi Arabia.

9. Children

Information about children travelling with their families is provided by a parent or guardian and used only to arrange their travel.

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the new date at the top.
