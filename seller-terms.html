Seller agreement

Rules for every store on AL MANARAH GLOBAL SERVICES · Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Who we are

AL MANARAH GLOBAL SERVICES (“AL MANARAH”) runs an online marketplace where independent sellers open stores and list their products and services. AL MANARAH is not the seller of the items listed by sellers.

2. Your store details

You must give your real name, business name, city and a working phone number, and keep them up to date.

3. One product per listing

Each listing must show one product (or one model with its sizes and colours), with its own photos and its own price. Photos of a whole shop, shelf or many different items in one listing are not allowed and will be rejected. A photo of your shop can be used as your store cover.

4. Honest listings

Photos, prices, condition (new or used) and descriptions must be true. Do not copy other sellers’ photos. Remove or hide items that are sold or unavailable.

5. Forbidden items

No illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or indecent items, no alcohol, drugs, weapons or tobacco, and nothing that breaks the law of the country where you sell.

Health & wellness: prescription medicines and any promise to cure, treat or prevent an illness are not allowed. Supplements, herbs, honey and oils must be described honestly, with their ingredients, and be registered with the competent authority where required (e.g. NAFDAC in Nigeria). AL MANARAH may refuse any doubtful health product.

6. Approval and control

Every store is reviewed by AL MANARAH before it appears; once a store is approved, its products are published directly and remain under AL MANARAH’s control. AL MANARAH may refuse, hide, edit, suspend or delete any store or listing that breaks these rules or harms buyers, with or without notice.

7. Dealing with customers

Sales, delivery, payment, guarantees and returns are agreed directly between you and your customer, and you are responsible for them. Answer customers honestly and on time. Ratings and reports from customers may lead to a warning or suspension.

8. Plans and payment

The Free plan allows a limited number of products. Paid plans are monthly and paid in advance to AL MANARAH by mobile money and start after AL MANARAH confirms the payment. Subscription fees are not refundable once the plan is active. When a plan ends and is not renewed, the store returns to the Free plan. AL MANARAH may change prices for future periods.

9. Promotion services

Featured (TOP) listings, home-page banners and the verified badge are optional paid services agreed with AL MANARAH.

10. Your content

You confirm you have the right to use the photos and texts you upload. You allow AL MANARAH to show them on the website and to use them to promote the marketplace.

11. Your account

Keep your password secret. You are responsible for everything done with your account. You can close your store at any time by contacting AL MANARAH.

12. Changes

AL MANARAH may update these rules. The current version is always on this page; continuing to use your store means you accept it.
