About AL MANARAH

Journeys arranged with care, transparency and trust.

AL MANARAH GLOBAL SERVICES is a growing international services company based in Abidjan, serving African customers with Umrah, Hajj, Saudi visa and international travel arrangements. We work with travel-service providers in Saudi Arabia to coordinate accommodation, transport and pilgrim assistance.

Our mission is to make pilgrimage and travel simpler, more transparent and more accessible — by explaining every step, cost and responsibility clearly before any payment is made.
