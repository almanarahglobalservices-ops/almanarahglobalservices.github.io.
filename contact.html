FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a product?

Open the store or product, then contact the seller by WhatsApp, call or request. You agree directly with the seller.

How do I pay?

Payment goes directly to the seller (Mobile Money, Wave, OPay, transfer…). Pay only after checking the product and the seller.

Who handles delivery?

Each seller arranges delivery or pickup.

How do I open my store?

Click “Open my store”, create your account, fill in your store details and add your products. You get your own link and QR code.

How much does it cost?

Opening is free with 15 products. To publish more, choose a monthly plan in your seller space. No commission on your sales.
